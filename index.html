Unit-V : Reading Comprehension (Unseen)

A wolf and a fox conspired to kill a lot of other animals and store up the meat for future meals. In the name of King Lion they called all the animals together and declared, "It is said that there is known throughout the forest a prescription for eternal youth and immortality which was handed down from the past, and it is being kept from us two and King Lion. On behalf of King Lion we order you to tell it. Anyone who refuses will be sentenced to death."

As self-appointed representatives of the king, the fox acted as the inquisitor, the wolf as executioner. The deer was the first to be called up, and of course, it knew of no such prescription. So the fox declared, "This deer is sentenced to death", and the wolf pounced on it.

The deer was followed by a goat and a pheasant and they were killed in the same way. Who would be next? Terror loomed large in the silent forest.

Then a small white rabbit hopped in front of the fox and the wolf and said calmly, "I know the prescription." Of course, the wolf and the fox knew there was no such thing.

"What did you say?" "Out with it then."

"The prescription is that King Lion must eat the heart of the fox and lungs of the wolf freshly cut from their bodies." Then the rabbit turned to the other animals and shouted, "Brothers, it's time to act! Cut out their hearts and lungs and offer them to the king." The other animals, united by their common hatred of the fox and wolf, pounced on them and killed them in no time.
